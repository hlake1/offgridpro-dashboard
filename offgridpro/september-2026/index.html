Top win this month

Organic search growth: sessions increased from 376 to 485, alongside a 33.8% increase in engaged organic sessions. More people are finding the website through search and engaging once they arrive.

What went well

  • Organic search grew compared with August, with website sessions up 29% to 485 and organic users up 18%.
  • Website engagement improved: engaged sessions increased 7%, and the engagement rate rose from 51.35% to 55.43%.
  • TikTok reached more people, with 3,700 viewers (+13.4%) and 2,300 new viewers (+23.1%). Profile views increased 31.5%, while shares rose 47.1%.
  • The Home Energy Storage (E) ad achieved a 20.69% click-through rate, making it a useful reference for future ad testing.

What needs to be worked on

  • Turning website visits into enquiries. Recorded key events fell from five to two, so we need to check conversion tracking and review the enquiry journey.
  • Paid search sessions fell 23.2%, while website sessions from organic social dropped 45.6%. We need to review PPC performance and give social audiences stronger reasons to visit the site.
  • TikTok views increased slightly, but comments fell from 11 to two. The existing videos provide a good foundation; testing more personality, stronger hooks and conversational content could help encourage interaction.
