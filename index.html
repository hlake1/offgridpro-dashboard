What Tweak Reporting does

1

Connects your data

Team members link their own Google account so reports pull real Google Ads, GA4, and Search Console figures automatically.

2

Builds the report

Account managers write the narrative — wins, focus areas, budget notes — and Tweak Reporting assembles it into a client-ready report.

3

Shares with clients

Each client gets their own private, always-current dashboard with their latest report and performance history.
