🗂️ Monthly Report — July 2026

Structured for Tweak's monthly review
The biggest win

Conversions tripled to 18 while Victron CTR more than doubled.

July delivered the strongest conversion month to date — 18 conversions vs 5 in June (+260%). Energy Storage drove 12 conversions; Victron drove 6. At the same time, Victron campaign CTR climbed from 4.83% to 10.23% (+5.4pp) — a step-change in targeting efficiency driven by campaign optimisation and a bolder content strategy launched this month.

📊 What influenced this month's performance?

Notable increases or decreases — campaign changes, seasonality, budget, tracking issues, client activity.

↑
Victron campaign optimisation. Refined targeting drove CTR from 4.83% to 10.23% — more than double — with 6 conversions this month versus 2 in June.
↑
Energy Storage keyword performance. "battery backup for home" (32 clicks), "emergency power supply for home" (26 clicks), and "best energy deals" (15.79% CTR, 2 conv) drove the bulk of Energy Storage conversions.
↑
Social surge across all channels. Facebook link clicks jumped from 1 to 464; Instagram followers grew 668%; TikTok launched with 5.7k views. Consistent video content driving reach.
↑
New keywords around 'off grid power systems / storage systems'. SEO efforts expanding the keyword footprint with 81 ranking keywords.
↓
Energy Storage CTR dropped ~3%. From 7.43% in June to 4.49% in July — while conversions increased significantly, indicating better conversion quality over volume.
!
Spend held flat (-4.4%). £474.83 vs £496.71 in June — more conversions at slightly lower spend is the efficiency story of the month.
